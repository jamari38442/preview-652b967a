About Us

Meet Rion Ferguson, founder of Canine Intuition.

Rion founded Canine Intuition in 2015 with over 13 years of experience in canine training. He’s a graduate of The Tom Rose School for Professional Dog Trainers, one of the top three accredited dog training schools in the nation, where he finished first in his class and graduated with High Honors.

Before returning to dogs full-time, Rion served as a Deputy Sheriff and was hand-picked for his department’s K-9 unit. That working-dog foundation shapes the balanced, customized approach he brings to every dog and owner today, from puppy foundations to serious behavior work.
